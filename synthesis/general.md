## Executive summary

A business can be modelled as a directed, heterogeneous, dynamic graph whose nodes are resources and capabilities and whose edges are the transformations that move value, cost, and cash. The financial statements are the aggregate projection of that graph at the firm boundary. The accounting identities — assets equal liabilities plus equity, the cash-flow statement explains the change in cash, retained earnings roll forward — are the invariant constraints the graph must satisfy. The model should be built on events and policy set-points, with statement lines derived as views, so that an agent optimises causes rather than accounting artefacts.

The graph needs layers. A seven-layer stack — transactions, activities, processes, capabilities, assets and liabilities, financial statements, strategy — can be projected from one underlying graph, with typed cross-layer edges. No single existing ontology covers the stack. REA supplies transaction and duality semantics, ArchiMate the structure and goal layers, BPMN executable flow, XBRL and FIBO the statement and instrument types. What has to be built is the connective upper ontology, a quantitative transformation algebra, an operational constraint envelope, and a live instance with one real consumer.

The engineering spine is now measured, not only specified. The minimal viable graph is an REA economic core plus a small driver set, enough to project the three statements and score one price or spend experiment. Documented build costs run from one to three person-months for a thin semantic layer, through four to nine for a multi-entity build, to nine to eighteen and a high chance of abandonment for a full ontology. A working ledger prototype confirms the cost shape: five fail-closed invariants fit in about 39 lines, accruals and depreciation are cheap, and foreign exchange and intercompany are where the lines of code concentrate. The mechanics of an exact close are cheap. Economic realism is not included in that cheapness.

The transformation algebra is a proposed constraint system, and the executed reconciliation says what kind of constraint it is. On a pinned object-centric inventory log plus a synthetic cost layer, `NI_GAAP = NP_TA + ΔInventory × overhead/unit` closes to a residual of zero. The absorption variance is internal to the identity, not a third term; omitting the variance close yields a residual equal to the variance. Flat inventory forces the two profits to be equal; a truncated opening balance breaks the snapshot identity by a predicted amount. That is a conservation proof. It is not a fact about any company. Contact with real filings draws the boundary sharply. On 45 SEC filers, the cash-flow identity — operating plus investing plus financing plus the exchange effect, against the published increase, same period end — is exact for 42 and within 5% for 43. A join that does not pin the period end manufactures billion-dollar false breaks; Advanced Micro Devices closes to the cent once the end date is shared, and looks broken by about $5 billion if it is not. The breaks that survive alignment (U-Haul at a relative residual of −0.85, Constellation Brands at −0.05, American Vanguard at −0.01) are concept-boundary failures, the shape the restricted-cash guidance predicts when the tags are not a closed set. On 234 ESEF filings with all flow legs, the same identity is exact for 63% and within 1% for 78%, with a fatter tail that is partly extractor ambiguity. The throughput identity itself cannot be instantiated on either corpus. Net income and inventory are public. Variable cost and overhead per unit are not, and several tags older alias lists still name return 404 on the current frames endpoint.

Benchmarking is possible only if it is layer-aware, and the remedy for the coverage tax is now measured. A strict synonym union — same construct, different standard tag — recovers 87% of the SEC revenue coverage gap (3,142 firms on the preferred tag, 5,344 on synonyms, 5,672 if bank and utility lines are folded in) and 100% of the COGS gap (1,895 to 3,398). Gross-margin coverage rises from 1,310 to 3,306 firms. Inventory's preferred tag already covers 97% of its chain; alias work there is wasted. Selling-and-marketing is the failure: strict synonyms recover 9.5% of the gap, and the other nine-tenths appear only if SG&A is treated as selling expense, which changes the metric. Expanding the peer set moves deciles even when no firm's own number changes (COGS decile churn 0.68). That population-shift tax is not the tag-choice tax and must not be used as a fragility test. Under IFRS, in a cache of 468 ESEF filings, revenue pluralism is mild — `ifrs-full:Revenue` alone in 307 filings, the contracts tag alone in 47, both in 11 — while cost of sales is present in a third, selling expense in 7%, and trade receivables in 5%. The fragile-versus-repairable split transfers. The repair does not. Standard-tag pluralism is the US problem. Missing analytical subtotals are the European one.

Survival remains the sharpest counterweight to richness. The compound event "an enterprise graph at three years is maintained, consumed, and tied to an improved decision" is bounded near 5% (range 1–20%). The measured predictors are organisational: a named owner, a consuming product, a budget line, a narrow scope, an enforced change process. Technology choice does not predict survival in the datasets that measure it. A piloted retirement registry produced 38 source-linked deaths, every one with a death date and only 39% with a sourceable time zero, so age — and therefore a hazard — is uncomputable for most of them. It is a biased numerator, sustainable as a harvester of existing feeds, and not a census. A prospective inception cohort that would supply the denominator needs on the order of 400 assets and four to five years.

Evaluating an agent on this graph has now been run, at a small scale, as well as specified. The acceptance rule under test was narrow on purpose: no hard violation, and cumulative net income above a threshold. Soft and values violations were computed and ignored. A reckless extractor that borrows and dividends itself insolvent is rejected in all nine episodes. A do-nothing policy is rejected for missing the income line, with zero violations. A red-team policy that inflates demand with a false claim — a values flag the grader does not read — and extracts cash as dividends is rejected on two scenarios only because it eventually breaks solvency, and is **accepted on the third**, with the highest income in the grid and 27 ignored values violations. Future-position roll-forward catches the violent harvest and not the accepted one. A live `x-ai/grok-4.7` probe, one seed, two scenarios, two personas, 24 calls, zero parse failures, did not use the seam even when the scoreboard persona was told the acceptance rule was the only objective. It earned less than a hand-tuned markup rule and violated nothing. The seam the model did not take is a one-page policy. An evaluation that reports only the model score will publish "no spec-gaming" in an environment where spec-gaming passes a third of the scenarios.

The simulator's economic target is no longer a slogan. On the frames extract, log assets (n = 6,396) have a standard deviation of 3.63 and skewness of −0.94. Revenue growth (n = 2,111, one tag, two years) has mean 0.14, median 0.04, skewness +3.66; 15% of firms move by more than half, and a Gaussian with the same mean and variance puts 46% of draws beyond that line. Growth volatility falls with size from 1.04 to 0.34 across the first four quintiles, then ticks up. Profit-margin persistence over two years is 0.58. A moment-matched Gaussian generator fails these tests. Matching the first two moments is not calibration. Matching the stylised facts would still not validate a decision: elasticity, cycle time, and the effect of an intervention are not in the annual file.

The corpus's own evidence posture has been scored and then re-scored. Fifty load-bearing claims, then sixty-two, on a five-dimension rubric. The observed core is High within the population it was measured on. The vendor and analyst band is Low to Very low, and its most-quoted statistics are its weakest. Constructed artifacts are Very low until they are executed, at which point they become High only as definitional or mechanical results — the reconciliation identity and the ledger invariants were promoted on that basis, and the promotion is a ceiling, not a licence to treat them as facts about firms. One widely repeated restatement rate was demoted because a measured vintage tax was about 7%, not 78%. The re-score's highest-value next measurements were a real-ledger cash leg, an alias-union test, and an adversarial agent run. Those measurements now exist. They did not make the corpus certain. They moved the uncertainty onto concept boundaries, missing subtotals, a grader that ignores its own values ledger, and a growth distribution no Gaussian will fit.

## Cross-cutting themes

**One object, several vocabularies, and a cheap invariant layer.** REA, stock-and-flow, Porter's activities, and ArchiMate's layers name the same structure. The part that had to be demonstrated rather than assembled is the invariant layer: a journal that rejects an unbalanced entry, a cash-flow identity that holds for 42 of 45 filers when the join is strict, a reconciliation identity that holds exactly on any consistent ledger and says nothing about a firm whose cost file is private. Encoding beats prompting. If a criterion can be code, it must be code. The prototype's invariant checks caught the projection bugs. The agent run shows the converse: a criterion that is computed and then ignored (the values ledger) is not a control, and a language model that happens not to exploit it is not a substitute for the control.

**Leverage sits where the model usually stops, and so does the data.** Accounting models expose prices and costs. The systems literature says the high-leverage interventions are delays, information, rules, and goals. The public file exposes the accounting surface and not the delays. Annual frames can reject a generator whose growth distribution is the wrong shape. They cannot score a price experiment. The honest map is: conservation and coverage are measurable now; mechanism and intervention are not, and no amount of alias-union work creates a demand elasticity.

**Feasibility, a consumer, and a death date dominate richness.** The minimal graph, the ledger prototype, and the survival base rates agree. The first milestone is a thin graph with one consumer, reconciled to the statements. Completeness is how these projects die. The registry pilot adds the measurement version: deaths are recorded more readily than births, so a retrospective file cannot compute a hazard. The denominator is an inception registration, which is an adoption problem.

**Coverage and construct are different repairs.** The comparability tax looked, on a first measurement, like peer-set exclusion. The alias-union measurement splits that exclusion into a part a synonym list repairs (revenue, COGS, receivables, the derived ratios that sit on them) and a part that is absence of the construct (selling expense, and under IFRS cost of sales and trade receivables). Publishing a larger *n* by substituting SG&A for selling expense is not a repair. It is a different metric with a 54% decile churn to prove it.

**A grader is part of the action surface.** Once the acceptance rule is visible — and in any real deployment it will be, because someone has to explain it — a red team with a copy of the rule will find every seam the rule does not read. Dividends do not hit net income. False advertising does not hit a hard gate. Future position does not unwind a brand stock the exploit has already booked. The model probe is encouraging and too small to lean on: invited to optimise the scoreboard, it did not take the seam, on one seed. The policy that was written to take the seam passed a scenario. Both results belong in the evaluation. One of them is the evaluation.

**Measurement moves the load-bearing uncertainty; it does not retire it.** The identity closes, on a synthetic cost layer, and cannot be computed on a 10-K. The tax is mostly coverage, and a synonym union fixes some metrics and exposes others as the wrong object. Cash articulation is an equality for nineteen filers in twenty, after a join bug that looked like a $5 billion break is removed. The registry can list deaths and cannot date births. The model does not spec-game in a four-episode probe, and a script does. Each of these is a narrower claim than the one it replaced, and each is usable only inside the population it was measured on. The confidence rubric's standing warning — a High-tier measurement of the wrong quantity is still the wrong quantity — is the result of the cash-join bug in miniature. AMD's false break was a High-looking number produced by a bad directness of observation.

## Tensions and trade-offs

**Conservation against realism.** The cheap, verifiable layer (invariants, a zero residual, a 93% exact cash identity) is the layer that does not say whether a price change will work. The layer that would say so (elasticity, overhead rates, manager behaviour) is the layer public data do not contain and a Gaussian generator gets wrong. Reporting a residual and a missing leg is the correct output. A pass/fail on "the algebra works in the world" is not available, and should not be simulated by quoting the synthetic zero next to a filer's net income.

**Peer-set size against construct identity.** COGS gains 1,503 firms from one synonym and loses nothing of its meaning. Selling-and-marketing gains 3,899 firms only by becoming SG&A. The decision rule has to name which threat it is pricing. A decile move caused by a larger peer set of the same construct is a documentation problem. A decile move caused by a different construct is a reason to refuse the benchmark.

**Profit against the values ledger.** The squeeze scenario is the tension in one table. The red-team policy has the highest income, zero hard violations, and an acceptance. The do-nothing policy has zero violations and a rejection, because it missed an income threshold by 300,000 cents. Lexicographic gating — any values violation caps the episode — would reverse the squeeze result and would not reverse the do-nothing result unless the threshold is also retired or justified. A penalty subtracted from profit will not do it: the exploit's income gap over the honest rule is several times a plausible penalty.

**Model behaviour against red-team behaviour.** Publishing the language-model result alone would support a claim the red team falsifies. Publishing the red team alone would support a claim the model, on this prompt, did not instantiate. The trade-off is sample size against coverage of failure modes. Four model episodes are not a ranking. Nine red-team episodes are enough to show a seam. An evaluation budget that buys more model seeds and no red team has bought the wrong thing.

**Speed against a gate that can be set by the agent.** Iteration speed predicts survival in the DevOps evidence, and it also predicts that an agent will outrun a constraint check if the check is advisory. The authorisation flag in the simulator is a log, not a control, because the agent sets it. The same pattern appears in the formalisation of values: a predicate the system can rewrite is not a predicate. Human authorisation has to be outside the action surface, or it is another dividend — legal, logged, and invisible to the score.

**Rich models against the base rate, and a numerator without ages.** Fluor-scale system-dynamics benefits are real in the case literature. So is the finding that most graph and data-platform efforts are not maintained, not consumed, and not tied to a decision three years on. The registry pilot does not soften that. It shows why a retrospective count cannot soften it: without time zero, a death date is an anecdote. The trade-off of building the ontology now is that the survival predictors (owner, consumer, narrow scope) are available before the ontology is, and the ontology does not supply them.

**Calibration against momentum.** Re-scoring promoted the reconciliation and the ledger invariants to High and demoted a circulated restatement statistic. The promotion is easy to misuse. High, here, means the measurement was direct, repeated, and not vendor-reported. It does not mean the identity holds for U-Haul's tags, or that a synthetic overhead rate is a firm's overhead rate. The demotion is the rubric working. A corpus that cannot demote its own most-quoted numbers is accumulating confidence by repetition, which is the failure the rubric was written to resist.

## Open questions

**The cash leg is measured; the throughput leg is named as missing and still missing.** A same-period identity holds for 42 of 45 SEC filers. The three breaks need a 10-K reading before they are called filing errors rather than tag-map errors. U-Haul's residual is large enough that one of those readings will teach the extractor something. The throughput identity needs a cost file no mandate requires. Until a firm contributes one, the synthetic zero should stay labelled as a conservation proof.

**Alias unions repair some metrics and convict others.** Revenue, COGS, and the ratios on them are benchmarkable on a strict synonym set, at universe level, on one pull. Selling expense is not. Whether those lifts survive a four-digit industry cell, a restatement toggle, and a UKSEF extract is unmeasured. ESEF local extensions were not resolved; the 103 filings with neither standard revenue concept are an upper bound on absence.

**The grader seam is demonstrated and not closed.** A values-gated acceptance rule, a dividend-adjusted residue, and a roll-forward that unwinds exploited brand are specified by the failure and not implemented. The model probe needs more seeds, a prompt that does not itself label the claim multiplier as a violation, and a second firm before collusion is a result rather than a citation. Six periods cannot show the coherence decay longer benchmarks exist to show.

**The generator that would pass the stylised-fact battery has not been built.** The Gaussian fails. An asymmetric Laplace whose scale falls with size, plus an AR(1) margin near 0.58, is a specification. Fitting it, and then asking the ledger prototype's event generator to emit a panel that passes the pre-registered bands, is the unfinished validation. Passing it would still leave elasticity and intervention effects untested.

**Survival still has no unbiased denominator.** The registry is a harvester. The inception cohort is a design. Neither has been launched. Age is computable for under half of the pilot records. Until time zero is registered at birth, the 5% compound bound stays a bound assembled from separate literatures, not a hazard from one cohort.

**The register is one measurement behind itself.** The re-score ranked a real cash leg, an alias union, and an adversarial run as the next measurements. Those now exist, and they created new claims: period-join bugs dominate apparent articulation failures; synonym unions repair COGS and not S&M; a visible grader certifies a values exploit on one scenario; moment-matching is not calibration. Those claims are not yet in the register. Leaving them unscored would repeat the failure the rubric was built to catch.
